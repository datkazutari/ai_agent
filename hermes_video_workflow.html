How Hermes Voice should shape your video

A creative brief tailored to your vertical serum reference

  1. MUSTConfirm the final brief: 9:16 vertical and 15–20 seconds.
  2. MUSTUse first-person narration only when it reflects your confirmed perspective. Never claim product ownership, use, or testing without your confirmation.
  3. MUSTBuild a shot plan from your supplied reference and approved or licensed visuals; keep source credits with the project.
  4. SHOULDUse slow macro pans, soft zooms, serum highlights, and natural pauses. Keep the refined studio mood of your reference without relying on a generic ad template.
  5. MUSTCheck the selected provider and its price first. Use a free preview path where possible; do not start paid generation without your explicit go-ahead.
  6. MUSTReview factual claims, caption readability, image credits, vertical framing, and the agreed duration before delivery.

This brief keeps the look personal to your serum reference while respecting your preference to avoid paid tools.

🚀How to use it

Open your private Lark chat with the Hermes Voice bot and give it a specific task. For example:

Help me plan a 15-second vertical serum video based on my reference clip. Keep the look elegant and personal, with clear captions. Start with a free HTML preview and do not use a paid video provider.

The video is still a next step: review the preview first. Generating an MP4 requires a working video provider and may involve a charge.

⚠️Notes

  • The video has not been generated yet; this page describes the Hermes Voice setup and planned workflow.
  • Use visuals you supplied or have permission to use, and keep source credits where required.
  • Free video services may have changing daily limits, resolution caps, or watermarks; check the provider’s current terms.
  • Review the script, product claims, captions, and final video before sharing or submitting it.
